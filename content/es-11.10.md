# ES-11.10 : મારો વ્યવસાય ચલાવવો

## ❖ Customer Service and Building Trust

### What Is Customer Service?
Customer service is the help and support a business provides before, during, and after a customer buys a product or service.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** Customer service એટલે ગ્રાહક ખરીદી કરે તે પહેલાં, ખરીદી દરમિયાન અને ખરીદી પછી તેને આપવામાં આવતી મદદ અને સેવા. સારી સેવા ગ્રાહકને યોગ્ય માહિતી આપે છે, તેની જરૂરિયાત સમજે છે અને સમસ્યા ઉકેલવામાં મદદ કરે છે.

### Why Is Trust Important?
Trust means customers believe that a business is honest, dependable, and cares about the quality of its products or services.

A business can build trust by:
- Giving correct information about price, quality, and delivery time.
- Keeping promises and meeting agreed deadlines.
- Treating every customer politely and fairly.
- Explaining limitations honestly instead of making false claims.
- Protecting customer information.
- Correcting mistakes and communicating clearly.

**ગુજરાતી ઉદાહરણ:** જો મોબાઇલ રિપેરિંગ દુકાન ગ્રાહકને કહે કે “આ કામમાં બે કલાક લાગી શકે છે” અને સમયસર કામ પૂરું કરે અથવા મોડું થવાનું હોય તો પહેલાંથી જાણ કરે, તો ગ્રાહકનો વિશ્વાસ વધે છે.

### Good Customer-Service Habits
1. Greet the customer politely.
2. Listen carefully to what the customer needs.
3. Ask questions if the requirement is unclear.
4. Explain suitable options, prices, and terms.
5. Confirm the customer's choice before starting work.
6. Provide a receipt or written details when appropriate.
7. Follow up when promised.

### Example
A customer asks a tailor to stitch a uniform. The tailor confirms measurements, fabric, price, and delivery date before starting. If a delay occurs, the tailor informs the customer early.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** શરૂઆતમાં માપ, કિંમત અને ડિલિવરીની તારીખ સ્પષ્ટ કરવાથી ગેરસમજ ઓછી થાય છે. સમયસર માહિતી આપવી એ વિશ્વાસ જાળવવાનો ભાગ છે.

## ❖ Simple Selling Technique: FAB

FAB stands for:
- **Feature:** A fact or characteristic of the product or service.
- **Advantage:** How that feature is useful or better in a particular way.
- **Benefit:** What value or practical result the customer receives.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:**
- **Feature (વિશેષતા):** વસ્તુમાં શું છે.
- **Advantage (ફાયદો):** તે વિશેષતા કેવી રીતે મદદ કરે છે.
- **Benefit (લાભ):** ગ્રાહકને તેનાથી શું ઉપયોગી પરિણામ મળે છે.

### FAB Example: LED Bulb
- **Feature:** The bulb uses LED technology.
- **Advantage:** It can use less electricity than many traditional bulbs of similar brightness.
- **Benefit:** The customer may reduce electricity use and running costs, depending on usage and product specifications.

### FAB Example: Computer Training
- **Feature:** The course includes practical spreadsheet exercises.
- **Advantage:** Learners practise common spreadsheet tasks.
- **Benefit:** Learners may become more confident completing relevant office tasks.

A seller should make accurate claims and avoid promising results that cannot be guaranteed.

## ❖ Cross-Selling

Cross-selling means suggesting a related product or service that may genuinely help the customer with the purchase they are already making.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** Cross-selling એટલે ગ્રાહક જે વસ્તુ અથવા સેવા ખરીદી રહ્યો છે તેની સાથે સંબંધિત અને ઉપયોગી બીજી વસ્તુ અથવા સેવા સૂચવવી. સૂચન ગ્રાહકની જરૂરિયાત પ્રમાણે હોવું જોઈએ; દબાણ કરીને વેચવું નહીં.

### Examples
- A customer buying a printer may need suitable paper or compatible ink.
- A customer purchasing a computer course may be interested in an optional practice workbook.
- A customer getting a bicycle repaired may need a safety light if their current light is missing or damaged.

### Ethical Cross-Selling Tips
- Ask permission before suggesting an extra item.
- Explain why it may be useful.
- State the additional cost clearly.
- Respect “no” without pressure.
- Never claim an optional item is compulsory when it is not.

**Example phrase:** “Would you like to see compatible printer paper? It is optional and costs extra.”

**ગુજરાતી:** “શું તમે આ printer માટે યોગ્ય paper જોવા માંગશો? આ વૈકલ્પિક છે અને તેની કિંમત અલગ છે.”

## ❖ Closing a Sale or Service

Closing a sale means confirming the customer's decision and completing the purchase or service agreement clearly.

### Simple Steps
1. Confirm what the customer has selected.
2. Repeat the price, quantity, service scope, and any important terms.
3. Answer final questions honestly.
4. Ask politely whether the customer would like to proceed.
5. Confirm payment method and delivery or completion time.
6. Provide a receipt, order details, or service record where appropriate.
7. Explain any after-sales support or warranty terms accurately.

### Example Dialogue
**Seller:** “You have selected one keyboard for ₹600. The total is ₹600, and it is available today. Would you like to proceed?”

**Customer:** “Yes, please.”

**Seller:** “Thank you. Here is your receipt. Please keep it for your records.”

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** Sale close કરતી વખતે વસ્તુ, કિંમત અને શરતો ફરીથી સ્પષ્ટ કરવી જોઈએ. ગ્રાહકને વિચારવાનો સમય આપો અને દબાણ ન કરો.

### Closing a Service
For a service such as repair work, confirm:
- What work will be done.
- Estimated cost and whether parts are extra.
- Expected completion time.
- How the customer will be informed if the estimate changes.

Obtain the customer's approval before carrying out significant extra work.

## ❖ Handling Customer Feedback

Customer feedback is a customer's opinion, suggestion, complaint, or response about a product or service.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** Customer feedback એટલે ગ્રાહક દ્વારા ઉત્પાદન અથવા સેવા વિશે આપવામાં આવેલ અભિપ્રાય, સૂચન, ફરિયાદ અથવા પ્રતિભાવ. Feedback વ્યવસાયને શું સારું છે અને શું સુધારવું જોઈએ તે સમજવામાં મદદ કરે છે.

### How to Handle Feedback Professionally
1. Listen without interrupting.
2. Stay calm and polite, even if the customer is upset.
3. Thank the customer for explaining the issue.
4. Ask questions to understand the facts.
5. Apologize when the business has made a mistake.
6. Offer a reasonable solution within your authority and business policy.
7. Explain the next step and expected time.
8. Follow up to check whether the issue was resolved.
9. Record repeated problems so the business can improve.

Do not argue, blame the customer, make promises you cannot keep, or share private customer details.

### Example: Late Delivery
A customer says that an order arrived later than promised.

**Helpful response:** “I am sorry the order arrived late. I understand this caused inconvenience. I will check what happened and tell you what we can do next.”

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** પહેલાં ગ્રાહકની વાત સાંભળો, અસુવિધા માટે યોગ્ય રીતે માફી માગો અને પછી વાસ્તવિક ઉકેલ આપો. જે શક્ય ન હોય તેવા વચનો ન આપો.

## ❖ Ensuring Customer Satisfaction

Customer satisfaction means the customer feels that the product or service has reasonably met the agreed needs and expectations.

A small business can improve satisfaction by:
- Understanding the customer's requirements before accepting an order.
- Providing consistent quality.
- Giving clear prices and realistic delivery times.
- Making it easy to ask questions or report a problem.
- Fixing reasonable errors promptly.
- Learning from repeated feedback.
- Asking politely whether the customer is satisfied.

Customer satisfaction does not mean every customer will always be happy. It means treating customers fairly and making a genuine effort to deliver what was agreed.

### Simple Customer Feedback Record

| Date | Customer issue or suggestion | Action taken | Follow-up needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12/10 | Delivery was late | Checked delivery process and informed customer | Confirm receipt |
| 13/10 | Asked for clearer price details | Updated price list | Review next week |

Use only necessary information and protect customer privacy.

## ❖ Classroom Activity: Mini Business Role-Play

**Objective:** Practise customer service, FAB, cross-selling, closing a sale, and handling feedback.

1. Form groups of three: seller, customer, and observer.
2. Choose a small business scenario, such as computer accessories, tailoring, repair service, or a training class.
3. The seller greets the customer and asks about their needs.
4. The seller explains one offer using FAB.
5. The seller suggests one relevant optional item using cross-selling.
6. The seller confirms the price and asks politely whether the customer wants to proceed.
7. The customer gives one question or complaint.
8. The seller responds calmly and offers a reasonable next step.
9. The observer gives constructive feedback. Rotate roles.

### Observer Checklist
- Was the customer greeted respectfully?
- Did the seller listen and understand the need?
- Was FAB explained accurately?
- Was the extra offer relevant and optional?
- Were price and terms clear before closing?
- Was feedback handled calmly?
- Was a realistic next step agreed?

## ❖ Quick Revision
- Customer service supports customers before, during, and after a purchase.
- Trust grows through honesty, quality, clear communication, and keeping promises.
- FAB means Feature, Advantage, and Benefit.
- Cross-selling suggests a relevant additional product or service without pressure.
- Closing a sale confirms the customer's choice, price, terms, and next steps.
- Feedback helps a business improve products, service, and customer satisfaction.
