# Topic 6: Managing Conflicts
## Module 6: Communication Skills

### Learning Outcomes
By the end of this topic, learners will be able to:
- Identify common causes of conflict at work and in daily life.
- Explain how communication helps resolve disagreements.
- Practise resolving simple conflicts calmly and respectfully.
- Use a step-by-step method to handle workplace disagreements.

### 1. What Is a Conflict?
A conflict is a disagreement between two or more people because of different opinions, needs, expectations, or understandings.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** બે કે તેથી વધુ વ્યક્તિઓ વચ્ચે વિચાર, જરૂરિયાત, જવાબદારી અથવા સમજણમાં તફાવતને કારણે મતભેદ થાય તેને conflict કહેવામાં આવે છે. દરેક મતભેદ મોટો ઝઘડો બને જ એવું નથી. યોગ્ય વાતચીતથી તેનો ઉકેલ આવી શકે છે.

### 2. Common Causes of Conflict
Conflicts may happen because of:
- **Miscommunication:** Instructions are incomplete or misunderstood.
- **Different opinions:** Team members suggest different ways to do a task.
- **Unclear responsibilities:** People do not know who is responsible.
- **Limited resources:** Two people need the same tool or equipment.
- **Delays or payment concerns:** Work or payment is late.
- **Behaviour and attitude:** Someone speaks rudely or interrupts.
- **Unmet expectations:** People have not clearly agreed on what should happen.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** ઘણી વાર સંઘર્ષનું કારણ ખરાબ ઇરાદો નહીં, પરંતુ અધૂરી માહિતી, જવાબદારીની અસ્પષ્ટતા અથવા ખોટી સમજણ હોય છે. તરત દોષારોપણ કરવાને બદલે પહેલાં હકીકત સમજવી જોઈએ.

### 3. How Communication Helps
Good communication helps people:
- Explain concerns clearly.
- Listen to the other person's point of view.
- Separate facts from assumptions.
- Find the actual cause of the problem.
- Agree on a fair and practical solution.
- Confirm who will do what and by when.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** સંઘર્ષ ઉકેલવા માટે બંને પક્ષે પોતાની વાત સ્પષ્ટ રીતે કહેવી અને સામેની વ્યક્તિની વાત ધ્યાનથી સાંભળવી જરૂરી છે. શાંતિથી વાત કરવાથી સમસ્યાનું મૂળ કારણ સમજવામાં મદદ મળે છે.

### 4. Five Steps to Resolve a Conflict
**Step 1 — Pause and stay calm.** Do not shout, threaten, or respond in anger. Take a short break if needed.

**Step 2 — Describe the problem using facts.** Explain what happened without insulting or blaming the person.

**Step 3 — Listen to both sides.** Let each person speak without interruption. Ask questions to understand.

**Step 4 — Find a solution together.** Suggest options and choose a safe, fair, workable solution.

**Step 5 — Agree on action and follow up.** Decide who will do what and when. Check whether the solution worked.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** પહેલાં શાંત થવું, પછી હકીકત સમજવી, બંને પક્ષને સાંભળવા, ઉકેલ નક્કી કરવા અને પછી તે ઉકેલ અમલમાં આવ્યો છે કે નહીં તે તપાસવું જોઈએ.

### 5. Respectful Language During Disagreement
Use:
- “Let us check the instructions together.”
- “I see your point. Can I explain my concern?”
- “I understood the task differently. Could we confirm the correct process?”
- “What solution will be fair to both of us?”
- “Let's ask the supervisor to clarify the responsibility.”

Avoid insults, threats, shouting, interrupting, rumours, and blaming someone before checking the facts.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** “તમે હંમેશા ખોટા છો” કહેવાને બદલે “મને સૂચના અલગ રીતે સમજાઈ હતી; ચાલો ફરી ચકાસીએ” કહેવું વધુ સારું છે.

### 6. Workplace Simulation A: Damaged Tool
**Situation:** Two trainees need the same tool. One returns it damaged, and the other accuses them of damaging it intentionally.

**Better approach:**
1. Speak calmly.
2. State the problem: “The tool is damaged, and we need it for today's task.”
3. Ask what happened and listen.
4. Check the tool and report the damage according to workshop procedure.
5. Arrange a safe replacement and discuss how to prevent similar problems.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** પહેલાં જ કોઈને દોષી જાહેર ન કરો. સાધનની સ્થિતિ તપાસો, સંબંધિત વ્યક્તિની વાત સાંભળો અને workshop ના સલામતી નિયમો મુજબ trainer અથવા supervisor ને જાણ કરો. જોખમી સાધનનો ઉપયોગ ચાલુ ન રાખો.

### 7. Workplace Simulation B: Payment Delay
**Situation:** A worker has not received an expected payment and is upset with the person handling payments.

**Better approach:**
1. Check the agreed payment date and available records.
2. Ask politely: “My payment for the completed work has not arrived. Could you please help me check its status?”
3. Listen to the explanation.
4. Agree on a clear next step and expected update date.
5. Keep a record. If unresolved, use the appropriate supervisor or formal grievance process.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** ગુસ્સાથી વાત કરવાને બદલે કામની તારીખ, કરાર અથવા ઉપલબ્ધ રેકોર્ડ તપાસો. પછી સ્પષ્ટ અને સન્માનપૂર્ણ રીતે payment status પૂછો. સમસ્યા ન ઉકેલાય તો યોગ્ય અધિકારી અથવા ફરિયાદ પ્રક્રિયાનો ઉપયોગ કરો.

### 8. When to Seek Help
Seek help from a trainer, supervisor, HR representative, or the appropriate reporting channel if:
- There are threats, violence, harassment, or discrimination.
- Safety is at risk.
- The people involved cannot reach a fair agreement.
- Serious misconduct or repeated harmful behaviour occurs.

Do not force someone to confront a person who may harm them. Follow workplace safety and reporting procedures.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** ધમકી, હિંસા, હેરાનગતિ, ભેદભાવ અથવા સલામતીનો પ્રશ્ન હોય તો એકલા ઉકેલવાનો પ્રયાસ કરવો જરૂરી નથી. યોગ્ય trainer, supervisor અથવા reporting channel ની મદદ લો.

### 9. Classroom Activity: Role-Play
**Objective:** Practise resolving a simple conflict respectfully.

1. Form groups of three: Person A, Person B, and Observer.
2. Choose a scenario: damaged tool, unclear job instruction, shared equipment, or delayed payment.
3. A and B explain their concerns respectfully and listen.
4. Identify the main problem and agree on one practical next step.
5. The observer checks whether both people were heard and whether the solution is safe and fair.
6. Rotate roles.

**Observer checklist**
- Did both people speak without interruption?
- Did they describe facts instead of insulting each other?
- Did they listen and ask questions?
- Was the solution safe, fair, and practical?
- Did they agree on the next action?

### 10. Quick Revision
- Conflict is a disagreement, often caused by misunderstanding or different expectations.
- Calm, clear communication helps people understand both sides.
- Focus on facts and solutions, not insults or blame.
- Agree on a practical action and follow up.
- Seek help when safety, harassment, discrimination, or serious misconduct is involved.

### 11. Assessment Questions
**Short answer**
1. What is a conflict?
2. List four common causes of workplace conflict.
3. Explain the five steps for resolving a conflict.
4. Why should people check facts before blaming others?
5. When should a worker seek help instead of resolving a conflict alone?

**MCQs**
1. What should you do first when a disagreement becomes heated?
   - A. Shout louder
   - B. Pause and try to stay calm
   - C. Spread the story
   - D. Make a threat  
   **Answer: B**

2. Which sentence is most appropriate during a disagreement?
   - A. “You are always the problem.”
   - B. “You never understand anything.”
   - C. “Let's check the instructions together.”
   - D. “I will not listen to you.”  
   **Answer: C**

3. If a damaged tool may be unsafe to use, you should:
   - A. Keep using it
   - B. Hide the damage
   - C. Stop using it and report it according to procedure
   - D. Blame someone immediately  
   **Answer: C**

4. If a conflict includes threats or harassment, you should:
   - A. Ignore it in every case
   - B. Use the appropriate reporting or support channel
   - C. Retaliate
   - D. Post personal details online  
   **Answer: B**
