
# How to Use the Scan Document Feature in WhatsApp

You can use WhatsApp's Scan Document feature to scan paper documents, convert them into a PDF, and send them directly to anyone.

## Step 1: Open WhatsApp

1. Open WhatsApp on your mobile phone.
2. Open the chat of the person to whom you want to send the document.
3. Keep your paper document ready on a flat surface with good lighting.

## Step 2: Open the Attachment Menu

1. On Android, tap the paperclip icon beside the message box.
2. On iPhone, tap the plus (+) icon.
3. A menu will appear with options such as Document, Camera, and Gallery.

## Step 3: Select Document

1. Tap **Document**.
2. Look for the **Scan document** option.
3. If it appears, tap it to open the document scanning camera.

Note: The menu may look different depending on your phone and WhatsApp version.

## Step 4: Scan the Document

1. Place your paper document on a flat surface.
2. Hold your phone directly above the paper.
3. Make sure all four corners are visible.
4. Keep the camera steady and allow WhatsApp to detect the edges.
5. If automatic scanning does not work, capture the page manually if the option is available.

**Tip:** Use good lighting and avoid shadows to make the text easy to read.

## Step 5: Adjust the Scanned Document

1. Review the scanned document preview.
2. Adjust the corners to fit the edges of the paper.
3. Rotate the page if necessary.
4. Retake the scan if the image is blurry or incomplete.
5. Confirm the scan when it looks correct.

## Step 6: Scan Multiple Pages

1. Tap the option to add another page or scan again.
2. Place the next page under the camera.
3. Scan each page in the correct order.
4. Review all pages before finishing.
5. Tap **Done** when all pages have been scanned.

If your version does not support multiple pages, scan and send the pages separately or combine them into a PDF using another scanning app.

## Step 7: Send the Document as a PDF

1. Tap **Done**, **Save**, or the confirmation button shown on your screen.
2. Review the document preview.
3. Tap **Send**.
4. Wait for the upload to finish.

Your recipient should receive the scanned document as a PDF attachment if that is the format produced by your WhatsApp version.

## Step 8: What to Do If Scan Document Is Missing

1. Update WhatsApp through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
2. Open a chat and tap the attachment icon.
3. Tap **Document** and look for **Scan document**.
4. If the option is missing, use your phone's built-in document scanner or Google Drive to create a PDF.
5. Return to WhatsApp, select **Document**, choose the saved PDF, and send it.

## Conclusion

WhatsApp's document scanning feature makes it easy to scan and share certificates, application forms, bills, and office documents without needing a separate scanner.

**Important:** The availability and exact steps may vary depending on your device and WhatsApp version.
