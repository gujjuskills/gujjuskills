## Introduction

Good news for EPFO members! The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) has launched a new WhatsApp service to make checking provident fund information easier for employees across India.

Launched on 1 November 2026 during EPFO's 74th Foundation Day celebrations, this facility allows members to access important PF account information through WhatsApp.

Members can check their PF balance, view their latest five transactions, access passbook information and receive guidance related to the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (PMVBRY).

The main purpose of this initiative is to make routine PF services more accessible and reduce the need for unnecessary visits to EPFO offices.

## What Is the EPFO WhatsApp Service?

The EPFO WhatsApp service is a digital facility that allows eligible members to access selected provident fund services through a WhatsApp conversation.

Instead of navigating several websites for basic account information, members can start a conversation with EPFO's reported official WhatsApp number and follow the available menu options.

This service is especially useful for employees who want to check their PF information quickly using their mobile phones.

## EPFO WhatsApp Number 2026

The reported WhatsApp number for the new EPFO interactive service is:

**+91 8796441800**

Before messaging, verify the number against EPFO's official announcements or website. Do not rely on forwarded messages or unverified social media posts.

Official website: https://www.epfindia.gov.in/

## What Services Are Available on EPFO WhatsApp?

### 1. Check Your PF Balance

Members can use the WhatsApp service to check their available provident fund balance.

This can help employees get a quick overview of their PF savings without first navigating the member portal.

### 2. View the Latest Five Transactions

The service allows members to view their five most recent PF transactions.

This feature can help you review recent account activity and check whether a contribution or other transaction appears in your account.

### 3. Access PF Passbook Information

EPFO members can access passbook information through the WhatsApp service.

For a detailed review of contribution entries and account history, members can also use the official EPFO Member Passbook portal.

Passbook website: https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in/

### 4. Get PMVBRY Related Assistance

The service also provides personalised guidance and assistance with requirements related to the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (PMVBRY).

This support is intended to help eligible individuals understand and complete relevant scheme requirements.

## How to Check PF Balance on WhatsApp

Follow these steps to try the new service.

**Step 1: Save the WhatsApp Number**

Save the reported official number, +91 8796441800, in your phone contacts after verifying it through EPFO's official communication.

**Step 2: Open WhatsApp**

**Step 3: Send a Hi Message**

**Step 4: Select Your Preferred Language**

Choose your preferred language from the options displayed by the chatbot.

**Step 5: Select the Required Service**

Choose the available option for checking your PF balance, viewing recent transactions or accessing passbook information.

**Step 6: Complete the Required Verification**

No. The new WhatsApp service does not replace every EPFO online facility.

The currently announced WhatsApp services focus on PF balance, the latest five transactions, passbook information and PMVBRY related assistance.

For other requirements, you may still need the relevant official service.

## What If the EPFO WhatsApp Service Is Not Working?

Since the service was recently launched, some users have reported issues such as verification failures, delayed OTPs and chatbot errors.

If you experience a problem, try the following:

1. Confirm that you are messaging the verified EPFO number.
2. Check whether your mobile network and WhatsApp connection are working.
3. Retry after some time if the chatbot displays an error.
4. Follow the official instructions for account verification.
5. Use the EPFO Member Passbook portal or UMANG app as an alternative.

EPFO may improve the service as it receives feedback and resolves initial technical problems.

## Important Safety Tips for EPFO WhatsApp Users

Your provident fund account contains important financial information. Follow these precautions when using online PF services.

- Use only WhatsApp numbers verified through official EPFO communications.
- Never share your UAN password or banking password with an unknown person.
- Do not share OTPs with anyone claiming to be an EPFO employee.
- Avoid clicking suspicious links promising instant PF withdrawals.
- Never pay an unknown agent to release your PF money.
- Use official government websites for sensitive account changes and claims.

Always verify the authenticity of a message before providing personal information.
