# Topic 9: Exploring Green Career Pathways
## Module 10: Green Skills

### Learning Outcomes
- Explain what a green career is.
- Identify green career areas and example roles.
- Connect trade skills to sustainability-related work.
- Identify a career area to explore further.

### 1. What Is a Green Career?
A green career contributes to environmental protection, efficient use of resources, pollution reduction, nature restoration, or helping communities respond to environmental challenges. Some jobs are directly environmental; other jobs become greener through better practices.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** Green career એટલે પર્યાવરણનું રક્ષણ, સંસાધનોનો કાર્યક્ષમ ઉપયોગ અથવા પ્રદૂષણ ઘટાડવામાં મદદ કરતું કામ. ઘણા પરંપરાગત વ્યવસાયોમાં પણ પર્યાવરણને અનુકૂળ રીતો અપનાવી શકાય છે.

### 2. Green Career Areas
- **Renewable energy:** solar PV installer, wind-energy technician.
- **Energy efficiency:** efficient-lighting or electrical technician.
- **Water and sanitation:** water-treatment operator, plumbing technician who reduces leaks.
- **Waste and recycling:** recycling worker, waste-segregation supervisor, composting assistant.
- **Sustainable agriculture:** irrigation technician, soil and water conservation worker.
- **Biodiversity and land care:** nursery worker, tree-care or restoration assistant.
- **Green construction:** energy-efficiency or sustainable-building support roles.
- **Environmental monitoring:** field assistant collecting observations under supervision.

Job titles, qualifications, and vacancies vary by location and employer. Verify current requirements before applying.

### 3. Skills Used in Green Jobs
Green work may require:
- Trade-related technical skills.
- Safe use and maintenance of tools.
- Measurement, basic maths, and record keeping.
- Digital skills for reports or monitoring.
- Communication, teamwork, and problem-solving.
- Awareness of safety and environmental procedures.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** ઉદાહરણ તરીકે, solar technician માટે electrical basics, સાધનોનો સલામત ઉપયોગ, measurement, fault finding અને ગ્રાહક સાથે વાતચીત જરૂરી બની શકે છે.

### 4. Connect Your Trade to Green Work
- **Electrician:** efficient lighting or solar-system work after suitable training.
- **Plumber:** repair leaks and install water-saving fittings.
- **Computer operator:** maintain digital records and reduce unnecessary printing.
- **Mechanic:** maintain equipment efficiently and handle waste correctly.
- **Agriculture learner:** support water-efficient irrigation and soil conservation.

These are illustrative examples, not guarantees of employment or qualification.

### 5. How to Explore a Career
1. Choose a field that interests you.
2. Read real job advertisements or training information from reliable sources.
3. Note required education, skills, certificates, and experience.
4. Identify skills you already have and gaps to improve.
5. Ask a trainer, career counsellor, or working professional.
6. Check local opportunities and safety requirements.

### 7. Classroom Activity: Green Career Search
1. Choose one green career area.
2. Use a teacher-provided job ad, training brochure, or reliable source.
3. Record job tasks, qualifications, skills, and safety requirements.
4. List two skills you have and two you need to develop.
5. Present findings to the class.

If internet access is unavailable, the teacher can provide sample job descriptions.
