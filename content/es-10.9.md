# ES-10.9 : મારો ગ્રીન કારકિર્દી નકશો - તમારા માર્ગની યોજના બનાવો

### ❖ What Is a Career Map?
A career map connects your current position with a possible future career goal. It identifies steps, skills, training, experience, and support that may be needed.

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** Career map એટલે હાલની સ્થિતિથી ભવિષ્યના કારકિર્દી લક્ષ્ય સુધી પહોંચવા માટેનો માર્ગનકશો. તેમાં કઈ કુશળતા શીખવી, કઈ તાલીમ લેવી અને આગળનું પગલું શું હશે તે નક્કી થાય છે.

### ❖ Reflect on Yourself
Ask yourself:
- **Interests:** Which tasks or environmental issues interest me?
- **Strengths:** What can I already do well?
- **Values:** Do I care about saving energy, clean water, recycling, or nature?
- **Work preferences:** Do I prefer fieldwork, technical maintenance, data, or community activities?
- **Learning needs:** What training or experience may help me qualify?

Do not choose a career only because its name sounds attractive. Investigate actual tasks, working conditions, safety needs, and qualifications.

### ❖ Build Your Career Map
1. Choose a career area.
2. Learn about the role and its requirements.
3. List your current skills and experience.
4. Identify missing skills or qualifications.
5. Set one achievable short-term goal.
6. Identify a trainer, mentor, or counsellor who can guide you.
7. Review progress and update the plan.

### ❖ Example: Green Career Map

| Question | Example answer |
|---|---|
| Career area | Solar-energy installation and maintenance |
| Why interested? | I enjoy electrical work and renewable energy |
| Skills I have | Basic electrical concepts, measuring, teamwork |
| Skills to develop | Solar components, safety, practical installation |
| First action | Ask my trainer about training and entry requirements |
| Time frame | Within four weeks |
| Evidence of progress | Find a reliable course or job description and note requirements |
| Support needed | Trainer or career counsellor |

This is an example only. Adapt the plan to your trade, interests, qualifications, and local opportunities.

### ❖ Make the Goal Specific
Instead of: “I will get a green job soon.”

Try: “Within the next four weeks, I will review two reliable job descriptions for solar technician roles, list their required skills, and discuss one training option with my instructor.”

**ગુજરાતી સમજણ:** “મારે green job મેળવવી છે” સામાન્ય ઇચ્છા છે. “આગામી ચાર અઠવાડિયામાં બે job descriptions વાંચી જરૂરી કુશળતા લખીશ અને instructor સાથે training અંગે વાત કરીશ” વધુ સ્પષ્ટ અને માપી શકાય એવું લક્ષ્ય છે.

### ❖ Classroom Activity: Career Map Presentation
1. Complete the worksheet individually.
2. Explain your plan to a partner.
3. Your partner asks one useful question, such as “What qualification will you need?”
4. Improve your plan using the feedback.
5. Volunteers share their next step with the class.

Respect that learners may choose different pathways, including employment, further training, entrepreneurship, or community-based work.

### Quick Revision
- A career map links a future goal to practical steps.
- Consider interests, strengths, work conditions, qualifications, and local opportunities.
- Set a realistic goal with a time frame.
- Seek reliable information and guidance.
- Review the plan as you learn more.

### Assessment

4. Write one short-term green career goal.
